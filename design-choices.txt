Branch: design/v1-dark-cinematic
Direction: Go dark and cinematic — deep blacks, sharp contrast, dramatic typography, feels like a premium Netflix title card

Color Palette:
- Void Black (#000000) — true black page background, creates maximum contrast
- Rich Black (#050505) — section backgrounds with just enough lift to separate from void
- Surface layers (#0A0A0A → #0F0F0F → #151515 → #1C1C1C) — a 4-tier elevation system for cards, modals, and raised elements
- Cinematic Red (#E63946) — primary accent, a warm crimson with more personality than Netflix red
- Hot Red (#FF2D3B) — hover/active state escalation of the primary
- Deep Red (#B91C2C) — used in shadows and glow effects for depth
- Gold (#C9A227) — secondary accent for premium touches: the "TBD" text, success states, select ember particles
- Pure White (#FFFFFF) — headlines only, for maximum impact
- Dim Text (#A0A0A0) — body copy, readable but doesn't compete with headlines
- Ghost Text (#555555) — metadata, labels, tertiary information

Typography:
- Headline: Bebas Neue (self-hosted via @fontsource) — a tall condensed uppercase typeface that IS cinema. Every movie poster, every title card, every premium streaming service uses this geometry. The extreme vertical proportions mean headlines can be set at 8-9rem and still feel controlled. Paired with 0.04em letter-spacing for breathing room.
- Body: DM Sans Variable (self-hosted via @fontsource) — optical sizing from 9pt to 40pt adapts weight distribution automatically. More personality than Inter or system fonts — the lowercase 'a' and 'g' have distinctive geometry. The variable font means a single file serves all weights (400-700).

Layout & Structure Decisions:
- Full viewport hero (100vh) — the title IS the page. No background image needed.
- Asymmetric card grid in CompactInfo — middle card offset 2.5rem downward, breaking the grid intentionally
- Episode cards alternate entrance direction (left/right) on scroll for visual rhythm
- 7rem section padding creates cinematic breathing room between acts
- Thin gradient divider lines between sections instead of hard borders
- Max-width containers (5xl for content, 6xl for grids) prevent the dark space from feeling empty

Animations & Effects (powered by Framer Motion):
- HERO ENTRANCE SEQUENCE: Orchestrated stagger — badge fades up → title reveals from blurred/wide-spaced to sharp/tight (1.6s) → accent line expands from center → subtitle fades up → CTA scales in with breathing glow. Total sequence: ~2.5s.
- PARALLAX: Title and subtitle move at different speeds on scroll (titleY: 120px, subtitleY: 60px), background scales to 1.15x. Creates cinematic depth.
- FLOATING EMBERS: 18 particles (mix of red and gold) rise continuously from the bottom of the hero. Each has randomized size, position, speed, and delay. Gold embers appear ~30% of the time for variety.
- SCAN LINES: 100 horizontal lines at 2.5% opacity create a CRT/broadcast texture across the hero.
- FILM GRAIN: SVG fractal noise texture animates in 8 steps across the entire page. Fixed position so it persists through scroll.
- SCROLL PROGRESS BAR: A red-to-gold gradient bar at the very top of the viewport tracks scroll position with spring physics.
- TOPIC CARDS: Each card slides in from alternating left/right with blur clearing. Chevron rotates 180 degrees with spring. Expanded content uses Framer Motion height auto-animation.
- COMPACT INFO CARDS: Enter with rotateX (3D tilt) that resolves to flat. Icons pulse-scale on hover. Parallax diagonal texture moves behind cards.
- SIGNUP GLOW: A radial red gradient behind the section scales and intensifies as you scroll into view (useScroll + useTransform).
- BUTTON CHOREOGRAPHY: Shine sweep on hover (CSS ::before translateX), Framer Motion scale + shadow on hover/tap. CTA breathes with animated boxShadow.
- CLOSE BUTTON: Rotates 90 degrees on hover.
- SUCCESS STATE: CheckCircle icon does a full 360 degree rotation on subscribe, spring physics.
- FOOTER: Staggered reveal of all four columns. Social icons lift 3px on hover. Links slide 4px right on hover.

What makes this version unique:
- The entrance sequence is what people will remember. You load the page and it feels like a movie is starting — the title materializes from blur, embers drift upward, the CTA pulses with light. It's not a website loading, it's a curtain rising. The scroll progress bar and parallax reinforce that you're moving through a cinematic experience, not scrolling a webpage. The combination of film grain, scan lines, and embers creates atmosphere that pure CSS alone cannot achieve.

What was intentionally avoided:
- No background images at all — zero external image requests. All visual interest is CSS + SVG + Framer Motion.
- No Inter, Roboto, Space Grotesk, or system fonts — Bebas Neue + DM Sans Variable is a deliberate, self-hosted pairing.
- No purple gradients, no blue/white corporate palette, no SaaS aesthetic.
- No rounded-xl pill shapes — sharp corners (0.375rem) reinforce cinematic precision.
- No scattered micro-interactions — all motion is orchestrated into sequences with intentional timing.
- No generic card layouts — asymmetric offsets, alternating entrance directions, and elevation tiers create hierarchy.
- No heavy libraries beyond Framer Motion — no GSAP, no Three.js, no particle engines. The embers are just 18 divs.
